## Live design feedback — November 7

The first #E7E5DB sample was too dark. The user chose to keep the later lighter warm white **#F7F6F1** after briefly considering a cooler gray. Accent remains **#95D4F1**. Controls should bulge outward with curved rims; reduce bright white glare. Card/button shadows must not be cut into square edges. The whole app window should be a rounded, raised surface. Use giant section headings between app-page groups on the Figma canvas.
